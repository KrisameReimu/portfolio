# Reference: Public Editorial Archive

## Use For

- Writing page
- article archive
- Awards page
- structured content pages
- Now / Roadmap pages

## Extract

- high-contrast editorial clarity
- journal-like typographic hierarchy
- slim borders
- spacious light surfaces
- minimal shadow
- clear archive structure
- subtle functional accent color

## Avoid

- making the site feel like a finance product
- overusing violet or blue accents
- dense fintech dashboard patterns
- CTA-heavy product marketing language

## Local Application

Use this to make writing and structured archives feel credible, readable, and mature.

Good for article metadata, category grouping, year archives, credentials, and long-form page hierarchy.
