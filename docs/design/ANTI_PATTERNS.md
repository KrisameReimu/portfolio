# Design Anti-Patterns

## Global

Do not copy external references exactly.

Do not mix many unrelated design systems.

Do not make every page visually loud.

Do not use AI artwork as a substitute for layout, typography, and information architecture.

Do not let raw Refero references override the local design system.

## Home

Do not repeat the same decorative density as the hero in every lower section.

Do not make the site look like a generic startup landing page.

## Writing

Do not over-cardify long-form writing.

Do not make Chinese text too narrow or too small.

Do not sacrifice readability for decorative treatment.

## Photos / Videos

Do not use heavy UI chrome around visual work.

Do not let background texture compete with photos.

Do not make captions louder than images.

## Projects

Do not make project pages too poetic.

Do not hide role, stack, problem, solution, and outcome.

Do not use fake metrics.

Do not use generic SaaS dashboards unless they represent the actual project.
