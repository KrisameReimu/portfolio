# Writing Page Rules

## Direction

Writing should use editorial archive style.

The page should feel like a curated archive of essays, reflections, stories, and notes.

## Priority

- readability
- chronological structure
- category / mood filters
- featured writing
- clean article metadata

## Typography

Use strong serif titles and high-readable body layout.

Chinese articles need generous line-height and moderate width.

## Avoid

- tiny body text
- over-cardified article list
- excessive hover animation
- decorations that compete with article titles
- dashboard-like metadata density
