# Design Tokens

## Principle

Use semantic tokens instead of one-off colors.

The site should keep a warm editorial identity while supporting clean software-style project sections and minimal gallery pages.

## Required Color Roles

- `--bg`
- `--bg-soft`
- `--surface`
- `--surface-raised`
- `--text`
- `--text-muted`
- `--text-faint`
- `--border`
- `--border-strong`
- `--accent`
- `--accent-soft`
- `--accent-ink`

## Palette Direction

- background: warm ivory / paper white
- surface: soft paper white
- raised surface: slightly brighter paper card
- primary text: deep charcoal
- secondary text: warm gray
- faint text: low-contrast gray brown
- border: warm gray line
- accent: muted brown or ink blue
- technical accent: subdued violet or slate blue

## Typography Roles

- display: hero and major page title
- h1: page title
- h2: section title
- h3: card title
- body: normal reading
- small: captions
- mono: metadata, tags, dates, code-adjacent labels

## Spacing Scale

- 4px micro
- 8px compact
- 12px small
- 16px base
- 24px internal section
- 32px block
- 48px section
- 72px major section
- 96px hero / large layout

## Radius

- small: tags and badges
- medium: cards and buttons
- large: hero and image containers
- pill: filters and navigation chips

## Shadows

Use soft, shallow shadows.

Avoid:

- giant blurred shadows
- glassmorphism glow
- heavy neumorphism
- random neon halo
